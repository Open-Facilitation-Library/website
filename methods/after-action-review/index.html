After-Action Review

Bring together the people who took part in one event. Use their accounts to understand what happened and choose what to carry into the next attempt.

OFL draft 0.1.0 · Six stages · One group discussion

Guide the discussion

Bring the original plan and any records the group can consult. Invite people who saw different parts of the event. The facilitator keeps those accounts visible, and participants can correct the record before it goes to anyone else.

  1. Frame the review

    Which event are we reviewing? Agree on its boundaries, who should be heard and who may see the record.

  2. Establish the intended results

    What did we expect at the time? Compare the original plan with what people understood their roles to be.

  3. Reconstruct what happened

    Build an account from observations and records. Keep conflicting recollections visible until there is evidence to resolve them.

  4. Examine why

    What helped produce the successes and shortfalls? Ask participants for evidence and record explanations that still need checking.

  5. Choose what to sustain and change

    Identify a practice worth keeping and an improvement to try. Ask who can take each action and where they can try it.

  6. Confirm the record and follow through

    Invite corrections, check that action owners accept their commitments and agree when the group will revisit the results.

Inspect the evaluation criteria

Suppose an observer recalls an alert at 10:10 and the log records 10:24. The facilitator should preserve both times and ask how to check them. Inventing a midpoint would hide the disagreement.

Adapt this review

OFL adapted the U.S. Army’s after-action review for civilian group learning. The original OFL protocol and evaluation text carry a CC0 dedication. The source notes, license and notice explain the scope and attribution.

Sign in to GitHub and create a fork of the method-specs repository. In your fork, open methods/after-action-review/, adapt method.md for your setting and update the matching criteria in evals/.
