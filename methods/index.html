Method library

Choose a method for the work your group needs to do. Each entry leads to a protocol you can inspect and adapt.

Learn from experience

  • After-Action Review

    Review one completed action together. Compare what you expected with what happened and agree what to keep or change next time.

  • Retrospective

    Look back over a period of shared work and choose a small number of improvements to try.

  • Appreciative Inquiry

    Identify what already helps a group thrive and design changes that build on those strengths.

Bring perspectives together

  • Collective Narrative Methodology harvest

    Weave accounts from several groups into a collective narrative while keeping each contribution traceable to its context.

  • Delphi

    Invite experts to reconsider independent judgments over successive rounds of anonymized panel feedback.

  • Dynamic Facilitation

    Reflect concerns, information, possible solutions and ways of framing the problem as a group works through a difficult question.

  • MosaicLab Deliberation

    Help a group of citizens weigh balanced information and develop recommendations they can support.

Develop a plan or proposal

  • Business Model Canvas

    Map how a business creates, delivers and captures value, then identify assumptions the group needs to test.

  • Navigate via Tension

    Explore a felt tension and decide whether there is an organizational need worth addressing.

  • Proposal Forming

    Work from a shared purpose, constraints and ideas to a proposal the group can consider.

Make governance decisions

  • Consent Decision-Making

    Surface and examine objections to decide whether a proposal is safe enough to try until its next review.

  • Governance Meeting

    Bring a team together to surface tensions, form proposals and make decisions by consent.

Adapt a method

Open the method folders on GitHub and check the chosen method’s license and source notices. Fork the registry to your account, then edit that method’s protocol and evaluation criteria in your copy.
